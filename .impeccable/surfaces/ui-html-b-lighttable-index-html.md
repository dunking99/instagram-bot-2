# Surface brief: Postroom redesign B, Light table

Mode: Operate. Whole app shell plus five pages (Find, Library, Videos, Triage, Fetch) and a persistent Lightbox. Owner-only, one Mac.
Deliberately independent from design A: light ground, top command bar instead of a side rail, favourites as a persistent tray instead of a page, tag-first Library with Sources in a sheet, single Videos view instead of two tabs, one-at-a-time Triage instead of three columns, activity as a dropdown instead of a persistent panel.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A photo editor's light table: images lie on a bright, cool, backlit surface and decisions are made by marking them, not by pressing buttons labelled with verbs. Refuses the dark sidebar dashboard.
OWN-WORLD: Cool paper-white ground (#eef1f3), pure white sheets, black frame borders around every image, vermilion grease-pencil red for picks, yellow for maybe, slate blue for rejects; ink-black type in a modern grotesque with a mono for frame numbers and edge codes; no shadows beyond one paper-lift, hairline rules, squared corners.
STORY: The owner types or picks what they want in the always-present command bar, scans a contact sheet, circles frames to keep (they fly into the Lightbox), and manages sources and jobs from sheets that slide over the work.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with brand, five tabs, one wide command field (also opens palette), activity indicator and model switch; below it the contact sheet of frames in numbered black frames with edge codes; right Lightbox tray holding kept frames; the composer options as a quiet row under the command field.
FORM: Chosen by the assistant (not the rolled direction, which was design A) for audience identification: curating frames is a picture editor's job. Roll seed key 2a6cd7f5 belongs to the A assignment.
SIGNATURE: The grease-pencil circle: keeping a frame draws a hand-drawn vermilion ellipse around it (SVG stroke-dash draw-in, once), the same mark family means maybe (yellow) and reject (slate X) in Triage and Review.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
